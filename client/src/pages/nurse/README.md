# Nurse Pages

Nurse pages are for clinic staff who assess existing patient records created by reception.

Current nurse workspace:

- `NurseDashboard.jsx`: Nurse page container for Dashboard, Quick Assessment, Patient Records, and Security.
- `NurseOverviewPage.jsx`: Clean dashboard showing the latest reception-created patient records.
- `NursePatientsPage.jsx`: Quick Assessment and Patient Records workflow. Search an existing patient, open the profile, then complete one assessment or send it to the doctor.
- `NursePatientRecordsPage.jsx`: Permanent patient records and previous assessment review.
- `NurseIntakePanel.jsx`: Current Patient Assessment form with vital signs, quick complaint capture, NLP summary, nurse confirmation, selectable medicine catalogue, and final submit/complete actions.

Patient creation belongs to the Receptionist workspace. The nurse workspace does not keep separate unfinished-assessment or sent-assessment pages.
